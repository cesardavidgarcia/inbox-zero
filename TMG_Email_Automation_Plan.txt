WHAT THIS SYSTEM DOES
----------------------
Every 10 minutes, the script wakes up, looks at the inbox, reads each new
email, decides what type it is, and takes the correct action.

It never sends anything automatically.
It never permanently deletes anything.
A human always makes the final call on replies and deletions.


================================================================================
THE 6 EMAIL TYPES AND WHAT HAPPENS TO EACH
================================================================================

TYPE 1 — TRUSTED AUTOMATED
  What it is:  Booking confirmation from Booking.com, Airbnb, Stripe receipt
  Action:      Label _Processed. Leave in inbox. No action needed.

TYPE 2 — SUSPECTED SCAM
  What it is:  Wire transfer demands, gift card requests, fake legal threats
  Action:      Label _SuspectedScam. Mark NOT important. Keep for human review.
               NEVER trash.

TYPE 3 — URGENT / WARNING
  What it is:  Real legal notices, IRS, permit issues, chargebacks
  Action:      Label _Urgent. Mark as IMPORTANT. Keep in inbox.

TYPE 4 — GUEST / CLIENT
  What it is:  Refund, complaint, booking question, damage, check-in issue
  Action:      Prepare a DRAFT reply (never send it). Label _DraftPending.

TYPE 5 — COLD OUTREACH
  What it is:  Unsolicited sales pitch, "I help businesses", "hop on a call"
  Action:      Label _ColdEmail. Move to archive.
               Auto-trash after 10 days if ignored.

TYPE 6 — JUNK / PROMOTIONAL
  What it is:  Newsletters, unsubscribe links, marketing blasts
  Action:      Trash immediately (automatic).

TYPE 7 — UNCLASSIFIED
  What it is:  Anything that does not fit the above
  Action:      Label _NeedsReview. Keep in inbox. Send notification to Cesar.


================================================================================
DECISION FLOW — STEP BY STEP
================================================================================

The script reads each email and asks these questions IN THIS EXACT ORDER.
The first match wins. It does not continue checking after a match.

QUESTION 1:
  Is it from a trusted platform (Booking.com, Airbnb, Stripe, etc.)?
  AND is the sender a noreply / automated address?
    YES -> Label _Processed. Leave in inbox. STOP.

QUESTION 2:
  Does it contain scam language (wire transfer, gift card, bitcoin)?
  AND is it also urgent OR from an external automated address?
    YES -> Label _SuspectedScam. STOP.

QUESTION 3:
  Does it contain urgent language (lawsuit, IRS, chargeback, suspended)?
  (and it is NOT a scam)
    YES -> Label _Urgent. Mark Important. STOP.

QUESTION 4:
  Does it contain guest/client language (refund, complaint, check-in, etc.)?
  AND is the sender NOT a noreply/automated address?
    YES -> Does it ALSO contain cold-outreach language from an unknown sender?
             YES -> Ambiguous. Label _NeedsReview. STOP.
             NO  -> Create draft reply. Label _DraftPending. STOP.

QUESTION 5:
  Does it contain cold-outreach language (saw your website, hop on a call)?
  AND have we NEVER emailed this person before?
  AND they are NOT a trusted or personal domain sender?
    YES -> Label _ColdEmail. Archive. Schedule for trash in 10 days. STOP.

QUESTION 6:
  Does it contain promotional/newsletter language (unsubscribe, special offer)?
  AND they are NOT a trusted sender?
    YES -> Trash immediately. STOP.

QUESTION 7:
  Nothing matched above?
    -> Label _NeedsReview. Send notification to Cesar. STOP.

_ColdEmail        Cold outreach in grace period before auto-trash
_NeedsReview      Unclassified, needs human eyes


================================================================================
HOW THE COLD EMAIL GRACE PERIOD WORKS
================================================================================

Step 1: Cold email arrives.
        Labeled _ColdEmail. Moved to archive.
        Script records the date it was flagged.

Step 2: Every day, a second script checks every _ColdEmail thread:
        - Did Cesar reply to it?             -> RESCUED. Keep it.
        - Did Cesar move it back to inbox?   -> RESCUED. Keep it.
        - Still in archive after 10 days?    -> TRASHED automatically.

Step 3: A daily summary email is sent listing what was trashed vs. rescued.

Step 4: Trashed mail stays in Gmail Trash for 30 days (still recoverable).


================================================================================
HISTORICAL BACKLOG CLEANUP (ONE-TIME RUN)
================================================================================

A separate function handles the years of old inbox clutter.

- Searches for emails older than 180 days in Promotions or Social categories,
  or containing "unsubscribe"
- SKIPS anything with client, urgent, or scam keywords
- SKIPS anything from trusted or personal domain senders
- Processes 50 threads per run, then automatically continues 2 minutes later
  until the entire backlog is done
- Sends a final summary email with grand totals when complete

Run this ONCE to clean history.
After that, the regular 10-minute script handles everything going forward.
